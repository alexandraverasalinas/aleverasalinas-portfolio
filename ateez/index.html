8 Makes 1 Team

ATEEZ is a South Korean boy group formed by KQ Entertainment in 2018. The group consists of eight members: Hongjoong, Seonghwa, Yunho, Yeosang, San, Mingi, Wooyoung, and Jongho. They debuted on October 24, 2018 with the extended play TREASURE EP.1: All to Zero.

About ATEEZ

✦

ATEEZ debuted on October 24, 2018 under KQ Entertainment, quickly making a name for themselves with an explosive energy that set them apart from day one.

Led by captain Hongjoong, the group has built a unique identity through powerful choreography, cinematic storytelling, and a sound that blends genres effortlessly. From their debut with TREASURE EP.1 through the ZERO : FEVER saga, the THE WORLD series, and now GOLDEN HOUR, each release pushes boundaries further.

What started as a bold debut has grown into a global movement. ATEEZ has sold out arenas across four continents, performed at Coachella, and earned recognition as one of K-pop's most dynamic live acts. Their connection with fans, known as ATINY, remains at the heart of everything they do.

History

⚓
Oct 2018

The Debut

ATEEZ debuts with TREASURE EP.1 : All To Zero and the title track Pirate King. The world meets 8 ambitious young artists ready to make their mark.

Feb 2019

First Win

ATEEZ secures their first music show win with Say My Name, just months after debut. A milestone that proved their rapid rise.

Aug 2019

First World Tour

Launch of their first world tour across North America, Europe, Asia, and Australia. Sold-out shows proved their global appeal from the start.

2020

The Fever Series

ZERO : FEVER Part.1 begins a new chapter. INCEPTION and THANXX as dual title tracks showcase their growing artistry.

2022

Guerrilla Era

THE WORLD EP.1 : MOVEMENT drops with Guerrilla, a bold sonic statement that shattered expectations and charted worldwide.

2023

Coachella & Stadiums

First K-pop boy group to perform at Coachella. Stadium tours follow, alongside THE WORLD EP.FIN : WILL with Crazy Form.

2024

Golden Hour

The GOLDEN HOUR series marks a new creative peak. Stadium tours continue, solidifying ATEEZ as one of K-pop's top live performers.

2025 — ∞

Still Going

ATEEZ isn't slowing down. With more music, bigger stages, and a fanbase that keeps growing, the best is yet to come.
